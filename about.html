About

What this is

Make My Layover Fun is a short list of places worth your time on a layover, picked by airline pilots and crew who pass through these airports every week. No sponsored listings and no endless directories, only the spots crew actually go to.

Where the picks come from

Places marked "Recommended by" were suggested by the pilot or crew member named on the card, and the badge links to their LinkedIn profile. Photos come from the venues themselves, from crew, or from Creative Commons sources credited on each page.

Before you go

Hours, gates, and prices change often. Always check with the venue or the airport before you head over. If you leave the airport, give yourself plenty of time to get back and clear security again. You are responsible for making your flight.

Privacy

This site does not use cookies, analytics, ads, or any other tracking. It doesn't ask for, collect, or store personal information. Your web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and reliability.

Links on the site go to other websites, such as restaurants, airport maps, and LinkedIn. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
